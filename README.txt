Follow your BMW or MINI in Homey as it happens: battery level, charging status, range, doors, windows, locks and climate, plus its location for your Flows. Use them to get a reminder when you park at home without plugging in, or to start something when charging finishes. A Car battery widget puts the charge level on your dashboard.

The app uses BMW's official CarData service, which is read-only, so it can't lock, unlock or change anything on the car. You need a My BMW or My MINI account: create a CarData client on the BMW or MINI website with the CarData API and Stream switched on, tick the data items to stream, then add the car in Homey with the client ID. The step-by-step guide and the list of data items are on the app's website. Not made or endorsed by BMW. Street addresses: © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL.
