Follow your BMW or MINI live in Homey: battery level, range, charging status, whether the cable is plugged in, location, and the state of doors, windows and locks. Use them in Flows, for example to get a reminder when you park at home without plugging in, to start charging-related automations when the cable goes in, or to log your mileage.

The app uses BMW's official CarData service, which is read-only: it can't lock, unlock, start climate control or change charging settings. Before pairing, subscribe to CarData and CarData Streaming in the BMW ConnectedDrive portal and create a client ID there. Streaming delivers changes within seconds; without it the app falls back to polling, which BMW limits to 50 requests a day.
